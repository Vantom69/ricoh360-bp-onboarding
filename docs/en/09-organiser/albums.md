# 9A. My albums

<JourneyStep
  number="9A"
  objective="Group your panoramas into clear albums to find them quickly, share them easily, and keep a clean workspace."

>

## Create an album

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:albums" />

1. From the web platform, open the **Albums** section.
2. Click **New album**.
3. Give your album a **clear name** (see the best practice below).
4. Confirm the creation.

## Add panoramas to an album

1. Select the panoramas you want to add from your library.
2. Click **Add to album**.
3. Pick the target album or create a new one.

The same panorama can be in **several albums** at once.

## Best practice: one album = one business reality

To stay organized over time, create an album per **business reality** and give it an explicit name:

<CardGrid cols="2">
  <Card icon="🏗️" title="One album per project site">
    Example: "Lecourbe Street project — Tower A"
  </Card>
  <Card icon="🏢" title="One album per facility">
    Example: "Lyon Part-Dieu HQ"
  </Card>
  <Card icon="👤" title="One album per client">
    Example: "Dupont Ltd."
  </Card>
  <Card icon="📅" title="One album per campaign">
    Example: "Quarterly inspection Q2"
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

::: tip Naming convention
When several team members share a workspace, agree on a **naming convention** from the start. It avoids confusion when the album count grows.
:::

## Delete an album

You can delete an album once it's no longer needed. **Heads up**: deleting an album does not delete the panoramas it contains — they stay in your library.
