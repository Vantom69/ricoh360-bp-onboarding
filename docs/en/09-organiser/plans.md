## Why use floor plans

When you document a complex site (building, warehouse, factory), a simple list of panoramas quickly becomes hard to read. The **floor plan** gives you a **spatial reference**: you know exactly where each panorama was taken.

It's especially useful indoors where GPS isn't reliable, and to share a structured overview with a client.

## Import a plan and position your panoramas

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:plans" />

1. From the web platform, open the **Plans** section.
2. Import your plan (PDF, image, architect's drawing).
3. **Position each panorama** on the plan at the exact location where it was captured.
4. Save.

You can position as many panoramas as needed, across multiple floors if required.

## Navigate from the plan

Once the plan is populated, your team (or your client) can:

- **See at a glance** all the points covered by your captures.
- **Click a point** to open the matching panorama directly.
- **Spot the uncovered areas** that still need to be documented.

::: tip Field tip
Before a new site visit, open the plan: you immediately see which points have already been captured and which are still pending.
:::
