## Improve your capture quality

A few simple habits quickly improve the look of your panoramas:

- **Clean both lenses regularly** with a microfiber cloth.
- **Place the camera on a stable support** (monopod, tripod) to avoid motion blur.
- **Take care of the lighting**: prefer even light and avoid strong backlight.
- **Use the self-timer** from the app so you don't end up in the shot.

## Common issues

### The camera won't turn on

If your camera doesn't react when you press the power button, the issue almost always comes from the battery or a firmware lock-up. Start by charging it for at least 15 minutes and try again. If the screen stays black, force a restart (see the table below for the THETA X).

::: tip Tip — RICOH THETA X
The RICOH THETA X has a **removable battery**. Keep a spare battery charged: you'll avoid most power-related interruptions in the field.
:::

### LED indicators — RICOH THETA A1

The RICOH THETA A1 has no screen. To know its state, rely on the LED indicators:

| LED behavior | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|
| Solid blue | Camera on, ready to use. |
| Blinking blue | Data processing or transfer in progress. |
| Solid red | Error or overheating: let the camera cool down. |
| Blinking red | Low battery: connect the charger. |
| No LED | Camera off or battery fully discharged. |

For the full LED code reference, see the RICOH THETA A1 quick start guide:

<SupportLink href="https://support.ricoh360.com/fr/manual/a1-prepare-02">RICOH THETA A1 guide — indicators and setup</SupportLink>

### Unresponsive touchscreen — RICOH THETA X

| Symptom | Likely cause | Solution |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| No reaction when pressing the power button | Battery fully discharged | Plug the camera into a USB-C charger and wait at least 15 minutes before trying again. |
| The camera was working, then won't turn on | Firmware lock-up or freeze | Press and hold the power button for 10 to 15 seconds to force a restart. If that doesn't help, unplug and replug the charging cable, then try again. |
| Black screen at startup | Touchscreen startup issue | Wait 30 seconds while the system loads. If the screen stays black, force a restart with a long press on the power button. |
| The camera turns on then shuts off immediately | Battery too low to operate | Charge for at least 30 minutes before using it again. On the THETA X, you can also insert a fully charged spare battery. |
| The touchscreen doesn't respond to taps | Temporary system freeze | Force a restart: press and hold the power button for 10 seconds, then turn the camera back on. |

::: tip If the problem persists
If the camera stays stuck after these steps, check the firmware or contact your account manager for a kitting reset (especially if the Simple Capture interface is involved).
:::

<FAQItem question="How long does the battery last?">

Battery life depends on your model and your usage. On average, count between 200 and 300 captures per full charge. For long days in the field, plan for an external battery.

</FAQItem>

<FAQItem question="Does my Business Pack include RICOH Care?">

Yes. RICOH Care is included in your RICOH360 Business Pack. The service gives you dedicated support, access to firmware updates, and camera replacement coverage as defined in the program terms. It lets you use your RICOH THETA equipment and the RICOH360 solution with greater peace of mind.

</FAQItem>

<FAQItem question="Is the GPS always accurate on the panoramas?">

GPS works well in open outdoor conditions. Indoors or in dense urban areas, accuracy can drop. To position panoramas indoors, use the **Plans** feature from the app.

</FAQItem>

<FAQItem question="Can I measure distances on a 360° panorama?">

Distance measurement is not available on every model or every plan. For precise measurement needs, get in touch with your sales representative.

</FAQItem>

<FAQItem question="Is there a limit to the number of share links I can create?">

The number of share links you can have active at the same time, and the options available (duration, password, rights), depend on your plan. You can disable a link at any time from your workspace to free a slot or revoke access.

</FAQItem>
