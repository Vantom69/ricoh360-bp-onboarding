# Welcome to your onboarding

You just received your RICOH360 Business Pack — welcome!

This space guides you from unboxing all the way to advanced use cases. Go at your own pace and come back to any step whenever you need it.

## What is the RICOH360 Business Pack?

The RICOH360 Business Pack is a **subscription offer** designed for professionals. It brings together, in a single package, the essentials you need to use RICOH360 day to day: a RICOH THETA camera, access to RICOH360 services, advanced features for managing and sharing content, and dedicated support to make your fieldwork easier.

In concrete terms, the Business Pack lets you capture 360° panoramas, store them in the cloud, organize them, share them with your team and partners, and tap into premium features as your business needs grow.

## RICOH Care: peace of mind for your equipment

RICOH Care is included in your Business Pack to support the day-to-day use of your RICOH THETA camera.

This coverage helps reduce the risks tied to using the equipment in the field. In case of covered damage or malfunction, the goal is to make the support process easier and minimize interruptions to your operations.
