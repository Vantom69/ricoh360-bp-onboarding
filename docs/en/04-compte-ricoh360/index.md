# 4. Sign in to your RICOH360 account

<JourneyStep
  number="4"
  objective="Sign in to your RICOH360 account on both the mobile app and the web platform — with the same credentials."
  outcome="Your account is signed in on both tools. Anything you do on one is immediately available on the other."
>

## One account for everything

::: tip Remember
The **same RICOH360 credentials** work for both the mobile app and the web platform. No need to create two accounts.
:::

Your sales representative sent you your credentials when your Business Pack was delivered. If you can't find them, contact RICOH360 support.

## Sign in on mobile

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:login-mobile" />

1. Open the RICOH360 app on your smartphone.
2. Enter your RICOH360 email and password.
3. Confirm: you're signed in.

## Sign in on the web platform

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:login-desktop" />

1. Open the RICOH360 web platform from your computer.
2. Enter the **same credentials** as on the mobile app.
3. Confirm: you're in your workspace.

## Why it matters

- Everything you capture in the field from the mobile app **uploads automatically** to your web workspace.
- Anything you organize (albums, projects, plans) from the computer is **immediately visible** on mobile.
- You have **nothing to sync manually**.
