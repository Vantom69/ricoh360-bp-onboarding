# 5. Connect your RICOH THETA X to the app

<JourneyStep
  number="5A"
  objective="Pair your RICOH THETA X with the RICOH360 mobile app via Wi-Fi so you can trigger captures and transfer your panoramas."
  outcome="Your camera is paired with your app: you can now take captures from your smartphone."
>

## Step by step

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:pair-theta-x" />

1. Turn on the camera and your smartphone, and make sure they're well charged.
2. Turn on the **camera's Wi-Fi**: on the THETA X, open Wi-Fi mode from the touchscreen.
3. On your smartphone, **open the RICOH360 app** and start the camera connection flow.
4. **Select your camera** from the list detected by the app.
5. Confirm: the camera is paired with your account.

<PermissionsBlock />

## Check that the connection is working

Once the connection is established, you'll see the following in the app:

- Your **camera name**
- Its **battery level**
- Its **available storage**

<ImagePlaceholder id="media:verify-connection" />

If one of these items doesn't appear, restart the connection.

::: tip Connection problem?
Most pairing issues are solved by restarting the camera and the mobile app. Also check that the phone's Wi-Fi isn't connected to another network.
:::

If the problem persists: <SupportLink href="https://support.ricoh360.com/fr/faq/ricoh360-app-connection-001">Troubleshoot camera connection issues</SupportLink>

</JourneyStep>
