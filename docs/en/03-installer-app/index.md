## Why install the mobile app

The RICOH360 mobile app plays three essential roles in your day-to-day work:

<CardGrid cols="3">
  <Card icon="🔗" title="Connect the camera">
    It pairs your camera with your account and triggers captures.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="📤" title="Transfer your content">
    It automatically uploads your panoramas to the RICOH360 Cloud.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="👀" title="Check your captures">
    It lets you view and check your panoramas before sharing them.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Download the app

Pick your store depending on your smartphone, then scan the QR code or follow the link:

<QRCodePair />

::: tip Compatibility
The app is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). Keep your operating system up to date to get the latest version.
:::

## On first launch

The app asks you to sign in to your RICOH360 account on first launch. That's the next step in your journey.

::: info Permissions to expect
On first launch and when connecting to your camera, the app will ask for several permissions (Bluetooth, location services, local network access…) needed for it to work properly.

The full breakdown by operating system is explained in step [5. Connect your camera](/en/05-connecter-camera/theta-x). No worries: we walk you through it when the time comes.
:::
