# 11B. Inspection and compliance

<JourneyStep
  number="11B"

## The 5 essential best practices

### 1. Capture the critical areas systematically

List in advance the **critical areas to inspect** (safety equipment, regulatory points, high-risk zones) and walk through every one of those points on each campaign. Consistency is the foundation of a reliable check.

### 2. Annotate your findings precisely

On each panorama, **annotate** during the visit:

- the **conforming points** you've validated
- the **non-conformities** you spot with their severity
- the **actions to take** or to escalate to a third party

[See how to add annotations →](/en/10-exploiter/annotations)

### 3. Organize by site / date / intervention

A typical organization logic for inspection:

- **One project per site** inspected
- **One album per inspection campaign** ("Q2 2026 Inspection")
- **A clear naming convention** by zone or equipment

This makes the **later review** and the **report sharing** with a client or an auditor easier.

### 4. Verify before sharing

Before any external share of an inspection panorama:

- Check the **content readability** (lighting, framing, sharpness)
- **Blur people** in the shot if needed (see [Blur people](/en/10-exploiter/flouter))
- **Review the annotations' content** — they're visible to the recipient too

### 5. Share only what's needed

Set up **share links** thoughtfully:

- **Limited duration** for external shares
- **Password** for sensitive content
- **Disabled** as soon as the share is no longer useful

[See how to share a panorama →](/en/08-retrouver-partager/)

::: tip Audit / traceability
For inspections subject to audit, keep the panoramas and their annotations over time. The platform retains your content history, which constitutes a traceable record.
:::
