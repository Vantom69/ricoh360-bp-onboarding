## Turn on the camera

1. Locate the **Power** button on the side of the camera.
2. Press and hold it for about 2 seconds.
3. The touchscreen lights up and the camera starts.

::: tip First boot
On first boot, the camera may ask you to choose a language and accept the terms of use. Just follow the on-screen instructions.
:::

## Charge the camera

Connect the included USB-C cable:

- one end to your camera,
- the other to a USB charger or your computer.

The charging indicator lights up. Plan for about 2 hours for a full charge. You can use the camera while it charges.

## Update the firmware

Before your first capture, **make sure the camera is running the latest firmware version**. An update improves stability, adds features, and fixes known issues.

The update happens through the **RICOH360 mobile app** (which you'll install in the next step) or through the **RICOH360 PC/Mac app**. If you don't have an app installed yet, no worries: you can come back to this step right after, or the app will offer the update automatically the first time you connect.
