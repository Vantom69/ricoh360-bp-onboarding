# 8. Find and share a panorama

<JourneyStep
  number="8"
  objective="Access a panorama transferred to the Cloud from the web platform, then generate a share link to send to a colleague, a client, or a partner."
  outcome="You can find any panorama in your web workspace and generate a shareable link in a few seconds."
>

## Find your panorama

<ImagePlaceholder id="media:find-panorama-platform" />

1. Sign in to the **RICOH360 web platform** from your computer.
2. Your panoramas show up in chronological order in your library.
3. Use the **filters** (date, project, album) or the **search** to find a specific panorama.
4. **Click** the panorama to open it in viewer mode.

::: tip Pro tip
Adopt a **naming** or **album-based organization** early on. As the volume grows, you won't waste time hunting for content.
:::

## Generate a share link

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:share-link" />

1. Open the panorama you want to share.
2. Click the **Share** button.
3. Pick the share options: link duration, password, access rights. Available options depend on your plan.
4. **Copy the generated link** and send it by email, SMS, Slack, or any other channel.

The recipient gets a direct link to the panorama, **with no account required**.

## Manage the links you created

From your workspace, you can at any time:

- **Disable a link** you no longer want to be active.
- **Change the validity period** of a link.
- **Check who accessed** the shared panorama (depending on your plan).

::: warning Security best practice
For sensitive content, prefer time-limited and password-protected links. Disable them as soon as the share is no longer needed.
:::
