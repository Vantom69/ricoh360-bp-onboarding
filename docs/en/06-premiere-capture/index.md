# 6. Take your first 360° capture

## Set up the shot

Before you press the shutter, take 30 seconds to check:

<CardGrid cols="3">
  <Card icon="🔋" title="Battery">
    Camera charged enough for your captures.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="🪜" title="Stable support">
    Monopod, tripod, or handle — skip handheld shots.
  </Card>
  <Card icon="💡" title="Lighting">
    Even light, avoid strong backlight.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Trigger the capture from the app

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:launch-capture-mobile" />

1. Open the RICOH360 mobile app.
2. Check that the camera is connected (see previous step).
3. Select **Photo 360°** mode.
4. Place the camera on its support.
5. Trigger the capture: use the **self-timer** so you don't end up in the shot.

## Simple settings to know

For this first capture, stick to the default settings and focus on:

- **Photo 360° mode** — active by default on your Business Pack
- **Self-timer** — to step out of the frame before the shutter fires
- **Support** — your monopod or tripod, stable and straight
- **Lighting** — a well-lit environment for the best result

::: info Advanced settings
Advanced settings (HDR, manual exposure, white balance) come naturally once your first captures feel smooth. They're covered in the [Improve your capture quality](/en/12-aide-depannage/) section.
:::

For more on the parameters available in the app: <SupportLink href="https://support.ricoh360.com/fr/manual/common-ricoh360-app-capture-02">Self-timer and capture settings</SupportLink>

## Check the panorama in the app

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:verify-panorama-in-app" />

As soon as the capture is done, the panorama shows up in your app. Quickly check:

- The **framing is correct** (no object too close blocking the scene).
- The **lighting is right** (not overexposed, not too dark).
- You're not in the shot (unless you meant to be).

If you're happy with the shot, it **uploads automatically to the Cloud** as soon as your app gets a network connection.
