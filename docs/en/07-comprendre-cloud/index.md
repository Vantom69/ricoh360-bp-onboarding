## The RICOH360 chain at a glance

<CloudFlowDiagram />

All your panoramas follow the same path. Your camera captures them, the mobile app transfers them, the Cloud syncs them automatically, and you find them on the web platform from any computer or tablet.

<VideoPlaceholder id="media:cloud-link-explained" />

## Why it matters

- **Nothing to do manually**: your panoramas head to the Cloud as soon as they're ready. No SD card to pull, no transfer to trigger.
- **One account everywhere**: the same RICOH360 credentials work for the mobile app and the web platform.
- **Your content follows your team**: everything is centralized in the Cloud, shareable, and collaborative.

## What stays on the camera?

Your panoramas stay on the camera until you delete them. That's helpful on sites without a network: you capture, you transfer later from the app, and the Cloud takes over automatically.

::: info No network at capture time?
Capture offline without worry. As soon as the app regains a network connection, your panoramas sync on their own.
:::
